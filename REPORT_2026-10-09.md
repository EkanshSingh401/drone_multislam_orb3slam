**Drift varies a lot across paths and simple, plannable quantities predict it** (landmarks in view,
how much of the view returns depth within 8 m, rotation, speed), unlike Δlogdet. But **OpenVINS's own
covariance does not track the real drift** (Spearman σ-growth vs drift: position 0.11 [0.06, 0.16], yaw
0.29 [0.21, 0.37]) — a drift objective would have to be learned/predicted from geometry, not read from
the filter. (Per-metre normalization partly reflects time-driven drift: slow windows accumulate more per
metre.)

## Step 5 — arming and post-landing disarm: DONE (arming fixed; disarm reliable with the final logic, PX4-first only partly)
**Arming cause**: the executor sent ARM only on ticks where `int(tp·20) % 20 == 1`, a 20 Hz wall-clock
timer tested against sim time. At RTF ≈ 1 (the lighter office scene) the index can skip 1 every second,
so no ARM was ever sent — PX4's log of the failed flight: "Ready for takeoff!", then neither "armed" nor
"denied". Fix: ARM every 1 s by elapsed time; PX4's command acks now logged. **Today: 16/16 flights
armed, each on the first command** (day 5 office: 3/5).

**Disarm** — causes found: with vision as PX4's height reference, PX4's height dead-reckoned once vision
was withheld below 0.4 m (it believed it was 0.8–1.2 m up on the ground), and the hold was keyed on
OpenVINS's height, which diverges upward after contact and *released* the hold. Changes (in order):
baro height reference + executor height-offset correction (PX4 vs OpenVINS height, EMA; the learned
offset reached 0.57 m in one 300 s flight — baro drift, tracked), latched landing hold, PX4
`ground_contact` accepted, and finally PX4's own local height within 0.15 m of takeoff and |vz| < 0.15
m/s for 0.5 s. PX4's land detector itself still does not report within the ~3 s before OpenVINS diverges.

| logic version | flights | disarmed by executor (PX4 / OpenVINS backup) | needed sim watchdog |
|---|---|---|---|
| baro without offset (invalid flights) | 3 | 2 (0 / 2) | 1 |
| + height offset (d6_check_zoff), then + latched hold (d6_check_latch, office 1–3, plain 1) | 1 + 5 | 0 + 3 (0 / 3) | 1 + 2 |
| + ground_contact (plain 2) | 1 | 0 | 1 |

With the final logic every flight was disarmed by the executor at touchdown, but mostly by the
