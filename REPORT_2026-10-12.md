## Step 1 — stereo in the prediction; absolute level
**Units bug.** `asi::predict_measurement` returns Jacobians in normalized image coordinates (pm.sigma =
σ_px / f); `information_random_walk` was given s₀ and q² in pixels, so random-walk tracks carried ~f² ≈ 2·10⁵
times too little information. Day 8's random-walk results (ranking 0.48 / 0.42 and the "48× too high"
level) were computed with it and are superseded. Fixed with a px→normalized scale (1/f) in both
functions, planner and drift_model; tests unchanged (unit-agnostic), 16/16.
**Stereo.** The scratch OpenVINS track log now includes cam1 (`[TRK1]`, same ids). On the 14 training
replays (validation scene) the cameras' errors drift together: per-frame increment correlation 0.3–0.7,
disparity error 0.08 → 0.10 px over 20 frames while each camera's error grows 0.13 → 0.38 px. Joint model
per axis: first sample Cov [[s₀², s₀² − s_d²/2], …], increments q² m [[1, ρ], [ρ, 1]]; fitted ρ = 0.97 (0.95–0.98),
s_d = 0.10 px; exact differenced information (`information_random_walk_stereo`, brute force 2.4e-14).
In MATH_TO_CODE.md (fork `31b524b`).

## Step 2 — plain-texture training data
Training-only scenes `texlevels_a`, `texlevels_b`: the validation geometry with each surface's texture
contrast scaled to 1 / 0.3 / 0.1 / 0.03 / 0 (0 = ±1 grey level, like office_plain's plain walls), two
permutations. 6 flights (2 frontier + 1 pose_cov λ=0 per scene; all clean: no VIO jumps, executor disarm),
replayed at 15 / 5 Hz: 65k tracks (median min-eigenvalue 5 vs 211 in the validation scene). Refit on
validation + texture levels (112k training tracks):

| held out | tracks | rate ranking | median ratio at age 1 / 5 / 10 / 20 |
|---|---|---|---|
| office (textured) | 45k | 0.48 | 1.13 / 1.03 / 1.00 / 0.93 |
| plain office (all) | 50k | 0.44 | 1.03 / 0.97 / 0.96 / 0.91 |
| **plain surfaces** | 3k | 0.15 | **2.91 / 3.22 / 3.60 / 2.89** (before: 2.28 / 2.74 / 3.00 / 2.39) |

With a hinge term for extra slope at low texture: identical. **Matched-texture comparison** (min-eigenvalue
< 0.1): training rate 0.10 px²/frame, gross 22%; held-out plain surfaces 0.44 px²/frame, gross 48%; even
at high texture (> 100) plain-office "plain" tracks run 0.086 vs 0.026. The excess is a property of the
plain-office walls, not of patch texture: large uniform expanses, features found only at their edges /
doorways and farther away (median depth 5–7 m vs 4 m), and the ray-cast truth is weakest exactly at depth
edges. **The 2–3× under-prediction is not fixed.** Building an office-like training layout to chase it
was not done (office layouts are held out); a rendered GT-depth check at feature pixels would separate
"real faster drift" from "truth artifact".

## Step 3 — deployable predictor (the number that counts)
Only what the drone has at the window start: the live estimate (path), the estimator's SLAM landmarks
(joint covariance at t₀), and the mapper's occupied voxels at t₀ (rebuilt from emulated depth at OpenVINS
poses, as the mapper does); predicted features = a 16×9 pixel grid cast every 2 s along the path into that
map (dedup 0.1 m, ≤ 1000), tracked by projection with stereo, FOV, depth and occlusion tests; rate from the
fitted model with texture and viewing angle unknown (training mean). 133 held-out live windows, 15 Hz.

| raw position drift | Spearman [95% CI] |
|---|---|
| stereo random walk, deployable | **0.04 [−0.18, 0.24]** |
| white, deployable | 0.07 [−0.16, 0.29] |
| stereo random walk, map at window end (NOT deployable) | 0.06 [−0.25, 0.38] |
| OpenVINS σ_rel | 0.17 [−0.06, 0.42] |
| geometry regression | **0.55 [0.47, 0.61]** |

Why: with the map at t₀ the predicted features cover only the part of the path that looks at already
mapped surfaces (in the first window they stop after ~9 s), but even with the complete map the ranking
stays ≈ 0. The oracle version's signal (0.52) came from the *actual* track set — which features KLT really
finds and how long it keeps them — which uniform surface sampling does not reproduce.
**Step 5's precondition (≥ ~0.4) is not met: stop.**
