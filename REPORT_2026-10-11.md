## Step 1 — the ceiling: what Spearman could a perfect filter reach?
Per window the filter's predicted covariance of the window error is taken from its own marginal sigmas,
σ_rel² = max(σ(t1)² − σ(t0)², 0) per axis (assumes the window's error is independent of the error at its
start; the cross-covariance is not logged; σ actually shrank over 25–34% of position windows). Drift is
then simulated from a calibrated filter, |N(0, σ_rel²)|, 500 times; Spearman with the filter's own σ.

## Step 2 — random-walk KLT error model, fitted on training data only
Training: the 7 validation-scene sensor flights replayed at 15 and 5 Hz with track logging (39,833 tracks
≥ 5 frames). Held out: the 9 office / plain-office flights (95,851 tracks). Per track the robust random-
walk rate q̂² = median_k |e_k|²/(1.386 k) (truth as day 7: first observation's ray into GT geometry).
Model: log q² linear in log texture (Shi–Tomasi min. eigenvalue of the 15x15 patch at the first
observation, from the recorded image), log depth, viewing-angle cosine, log image motion per frame, log
frame interval; Huber-robust least squares.

Standardized coefficients: image motion per frame **+0.79**, texture −0.33, viewing angle −0.24, frame
interval +0.15, depth −0.04; median rate 0.018 px² per frame.

**The random-walk shape holds out of sample** (ratio flat at ≈ 1 from age 2 to 20; a white error would fall

## Step 3 — information of a track under the random-walk model
Derivation (MATH_TO_CODE.md, active_slam_information `e12c376`): with e₀ ~ N(0, s₀²), increments ~ N(0, q² m_k),
differencing the measurements whitens the noise exactly, so
J_RW = H₀ᵀH₀/s₀² + Σ_k (H_k − H_{k−1})ᵀ(H_k − H_{k−1})/(q² m_k), versus J_white = Σ_k H_kᵀH_k/σ². Repeated,
similar views (hover, slow motion, high frame rate) add almost nothing after the first sample; information
comes from how much the geometry changes along a track, relative to the drift accumulated meanwhile.
Code: `information_random_walk` (candidate_scoring.hpp); planner option `meas_model: random_walk` with
`rw_sigma0`, `rw_q2`, `rw_frame_rate` (default `white` = old behaviour; tracks = landmark × camera over
consecutive waypoints). Tests: brute-force H^T Σ_e⁻¹ H, relative error 7e-16; q² → ∞ limit; Monte Carlo
(20,000 draws) covariance within 1.3%. The planner integration compiles; it has not been flown (as asked).

## Step 4 — does drift predicted by the corrected model rank realized drift? (report only)
New tool `drift_model`: per 20 s window, the planner's linear model (IMU prior from the flight's joint
covariance at t₀, static propagation, clones at 5 Hz) with every actually tracked feature, landmark points
from the GT ray cast (oracle geometry, so only the measurement model differs), each track as white 1 px
samples or as a random walk with its predicted q² (s₀ = 0.5 px); output the predicted relative-pose
covariance first→last clone. Held out: 247 windows of the 9 office / plain-office replays.

**Raw per window** (Spearman with realized drift [95% CI over flights]):

| predictor | position 15 Hz | position 5 Hz | yaw 15 Hz | yaw 5 Hz |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| random-walk model | **0.48 [0.30, 0.60]** | **0.42 [0.21, 0.49]** | 0.01 | 0.07 |
| white model, same tool | 0.27 [−0.05, 0.58] | 0.25 [−0.08, 0.53] | 0.18 | 0.03 |
| (a) OpenVINS σ_rel | 0.19 [−0.06, 0.44] | 0.14 [−0.10, 0.40] | 0.03 | 0.18 |
| (b) geometry regression (trained on validation only) | 0.54 [0.44, 0.61] | 0.43 [0.20, 0.55] | 0.00 | 0.07 |
| path length alone | −0.07 | −0.01 | 0.28 | 0.06 |
| (c) ceiling, calibrated filter with OpenVINS's σ | 0.92 | 0.89 | 0.50 | 0.52 |

Per metre (days 6–7 metric) everything clusters at 0.68–0.77 for position, at the level of 1/path alone
(0.63 / 0.68) — that form cannot separate the predictors.
Absolute levels (medians, position per window): realized 1.8–1.9 cm, white model 1.9–2.5 cm, OpenVINS
3.1–3.4 cm, **random-walk model 84–86 cm** — the tool is monocular (cam0 tracks only; OpenVINS uses stereo),
and under the random-walk model translation without stereo is barely constrained, so the level is
unusable while the ranking is informative. s₀ = 0.3 / 1.0 px gives the same ranks (±0.005).

**Answer**: for position, modelling KLT error as a texture/motion-dependent random walk roughly doubles
how well a filter-style prediction ranks real drift (0.42–0.48 vs 0.25–0.27 white, 0.14–0.19 OpenVINS) and
brings it level with the geometry baseline (0.43–0.54), still well under the ceiling. For yaw, none of the
candidates ranks raw drift (≤ 0.18) although 0.50 would be achievable.
